## Overview

Kindred Photos supports household accounts so family members can browse the photo library without needing their own Flickr account. Each installation has one owner and can have multiple admins and members. Everyone signs in to the household’s own server.

## Roles

| Role | Capabilities |
|------|-------------|
| **Owner** | Admin permissions, plus ownership transfer and household deletion |
| **Admin** | Connect Flickr, run scans, label/merge/dismiss clusters, delete photos, manage members, create invites, intelligence settings |
| **Member** | Browse all photos, search, view clusters, timeline, locations, events, upload photos |

Admins can change roles in Settings. A household can have multiple owners. Each owner can transfer their own ownership to another member. See [Roles](/docs/household/roles) for the full comparison.

## Inviting members

You can invite family members by email (using the Resend integration) or by sharing a manual invite code. See [Invites](/docs/household/invites) for the full setup guide.

The quick version:

1. Go to **Settings** (gear icon in the user menu)
2. Click **Invite member** (email) or **Create invite code** (manual)
3. Share the invite code or link with a family member

Invite codes are 8 characters, expire after 7 days, and can only be used once.

## Member registration

Members can register at `/join` or use the direct invite link:

```
https://your-kindred.com/join?code=ABC12345
```

They'll create a username, display name, and password. No Flickr account needed.

## Avatars

Every household member can set a profile avatar. Kindred Photos lets you pick a photo from your own library and crop it to use as your profile picture.

To set your avatar:

1. Click your avatar (or initials) in the top navigation
2. Select **Edit profile**
3. Choose a photo from the library or upload a new one
4. Crop and save

Avatars appear throughout the app -- in the navigation bar, notification messages, and household member lists.

## Managing members

From the Settings page, admins can:

- View all household members and their roles
- Promote a member to admin or demote an admin to member
- Remove members (immediately revokes access)
- View and revoke active invite codes

## Backups

Uploads are saved to the household's NAS. If its admin enables a Flickr
connection, supported uploads are also queued for the household’s one Flickr
destination. Members use their own accounts and have their own favorites.

## Delete your account or household

Open **Settings → Account → Manage account** on iOS or the web. Confirm your
identity, then use **Delete Account** to remove your profile, credentials,
favorites, and share links. The household’s shared media stays in its storage.

If you own the household, transfer ownership to another member before deleting
your own account. To close the installation for everyone, use **Delete Household**.
That removes the Kindred accounts, catalog, and connections. The server operator
manages the files held on the NAS, in R2, and in Flickr. A new Kindred
installation needs a new database.
