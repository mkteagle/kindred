Kindred Photos lets you browse, search, and back up the photos and videos on your own Kindred server.

BROWSE YOUR LIBRARY
Scroll through photos by date, open a photo full screen, and return to your favorites and albums. iPad shows more of your collection on a larger screen and supports both light and dark mode.

FIND A PHOTO
Search by subject or narrow results by month. Browse groups of people and pets, then open the photos you want to keep, share, or export.

BACK UP TO YOUR SERVER
Back up photos and videos from your devices. Resumable uploads pick up after interruptions. See what has reached your NAS and check your Flickr copy if your household uses Flickr.

CONNECT YOUR DEVICES
Use a pairing code from your Kindred server to connect another device. Manage household settings from the web, and set a profile photo from your library, an upload, or an available Google profile photo.

Kindred is self-hosted. Connecting and backing up a real library requires a Kindred server. Flickr and Google are optional. The in-app demo includes a sample library you can explore without an account.
