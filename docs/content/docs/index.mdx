## What is Kindred?

Kindred turns your Flickr photo library into something a household can actually use. It automatically detects faces, pets, and vehicles in your photos, clusters them into people and animals, and lets you browse by person, location, event, or timeline.

Unlike cloud photo services, Kindred runs on your own server. Your photos stay on Flickr — Kindred just indexes and organizes them. No photo data is stored on the Kindred server itself.

## Features

- **Face recognition** — ArcFace-powered detection and clustering, automatically groups photos by person
- **Pet & vehicle detection** — YOLOv8 detects animals and vehicles
- **Visual search** — CLIP-powered text search ("kids at the beach") and similar-photo search
- **Timeline & locations** — Browse photos by date or on a map
- **Events** — Automatic event detection from time/location clusters
- **Review mode** — Keyboard-driven triage for quickly naming and organizing people
- **Manual tagging** — Draw bounding boxes to tag missed faces
- **Custom avatars** — Pick the best photo and crop for each person's card
- **Duplicates** — Find and manage duplicate photos
- **Color search** — Find photos by dominant color
- **OCR** — Detect text in photos

## Architecture

```
Flickr (photo storage)
   |
   v
Kindred Backend (FastAPI + PostgreSQL/pgvector)
   |                    |
   v                    v
Kindred Web (Next.js)   Kindred iOS (SwiftUI)
```

- **Backend** — Python FastAPI service with InsightFace, YOLOv8, and CLIP models. Runs in Docker.
- **Database** — PostgreSQL with pgvector for embedding similarity search
- **Web frontend** — Next.js app, can be deployed on Vercel or self-hosted
- **iOS app** — Native SwiftUI app connecting to your backend
- **Photo storage** — Flickr. Kindred never stores your photos, only metadata and embeddings.
